# Supplementary Figure legends

## Supplementary Figure S1. Screen-wide signal-level colocalization and model quality control

**A**, Complete results for all 23 prespecified PBC locus–gene–cell combinations under the strict shared-signal prior `p12=1×10^-6`. Point color denotes the locked evidence class and point size reflects the number of overlapping variants in the paired 95% credible sets. The non-convergent NFKB1 estimate is retained only as an audit trace and is not inferential. **B**, Prior sensitivity of the same 23 combinations at `p12=1×10^-6`, `5×10^-6` and `1×10^-5`. Final evidence classes were assigned under the strict prior; a relaxed-prior increase did not upgrade the locked class. **C**, Disease-side SuSiE results across the 10 prioritized PBC loci. Point size denotes the number of 95% credible sets and color denotes minimum credible-set purity. All disease models converged. The 1p31.3 locus was the only multi-signal region, with three credible sets of 2, 3 and 2 variants in the L=10 model. **D**, Model-convergence audit. Twenty-one of 23 eQTL L=10 models converged. The two failures underwent a prespecified L=3 review: IL12RB2/NK converged and was included, whereas NFKB1/CD4 TCM remained non-convergent and was excluded. At the strict prior, the screen contained 6 strong, 12 candidate, 3 exploratory, 1 no-support and 1 non-convergent excluded combination. Combinations sharing one PBC locus are not independent discoveries.

## Supplementary Figure S2. Local signed-LD and SuSiE quality control across 10 PBC loci

**A**, Comparison of maximum disease SuSiE posterior inclusion probabilities under L=3 and L=10. All 20 models converged, and the absolute L=10-minus-L=3 difference did not exceed 0.010 at any locus. **B**, Audit of the exact 1000 Genomes European signed dosage-correlation matrices and variant order used for fine-mapping. All 10 matrices passed file-size, finite-value, diagonal, symmetry, variant-order and allele-alignment checks. **C**, Signed-LD heatmaps restricted to the union of L=10 credible-set variants at each locus. Blue represents negative signed correlation and is compatible with high absolute credible-set purity. **D**, Minimum absolute within-credible-set correlation, credible-set size and top PIP, together with RSS diagnostic boundaries. The 1p31.3 locus was the only multi-signal locus, whereas 2p25.1 had the weakest minimum credible-set purity and the 5q31.3, 4q24 and 7q34 loci remained broad. External reference LD supports summary-statistic fine-mapping but does not replace study-specific LD or individual-level conditional analysis.

## Supplementary Figure S3. Competing genes and locus-level downgrading

**A**, Strict-prior signal-level colocalization results for prespecified competitors at 2p25.1, 1q23.1, 18q22.2 and 17q12. Multiple cell-type combinations at the same locus are shown but are not counted as independent discoveries. **B**, Study-level external eQTL colocalization audit for the FCRL3/FCRL3-AS1 and CD226/DOK6 regulatory units. Blank cells denote unavailable gene-context pairs rather than negative results. **C**, FinnGen evidence at the prespecified lead-variant and regional levels for IL12RB2, the FCRL3 unit and the CD226/DOK6 unit. Only IL12RB2 passed both prespecified-lead and regional replication. The primary 18q22.2 lead was removed by FinnGen genotype quality control. **D**, Locked locus-level interpretation and downgrade rationale. Current evidence favours LINC00299 over ID2 at 2p25.1, but does not establish a validated therapeutic target; FCRL3/FCRL3-AS1, CD226/DOK6 and ORMDL3/GSDMB remain unresolved within-locus regulatory units. Signal sharing does not by itself identify a causal transcript or prove druggability.

## Supplementary Figure S4. Independent disease replication and regional compatibility at IL12RB2

**A**, Allele-aligned effects of rs6679356-C across the discovery PBC GWAS, FinnGen and UK Biobank. Positive coefficients consistently indicate increased PBC risk. Error bars are 95% confidence intervals; the UK Biobank standard error was reconstructed from the reported two-sided P value. **B**, FinnGen replication ladder separating the prespecified variant, regional top association and gene-level test. The gene test passed BH-FDR but not the genome-wide Bonferroni threshold. **C**, FinnGen 95% credible set under a single-causal Wakefield approximate-Bayes-factor model with prior standard deviation 0.20 on the log-odds scale. The 12 largest individual posterior probabilities and the remaining credible-set mass are displayed. **D**, FinnGen–external-eQTL BF–BF colocalization sensitivity across three shared-signal priors, with 95% credible-set overlap shown alongside. Perez NK and GTEx blood were compatible with the FinnGen signal, whereas Nathan Th1 was an informative negative context. BF–BF is a single-causal approximation and is not FinnGen `coloc.susie`, because complete unthresholded Finnish signed LD was unavailable; the public 19-variant official matrix was suitable only for structural checks.

## Supplementary Figure S5. Splicing and cross-platform protein evidence define a mediation boundary

**A**, Audit of IL12RB2 splice-QTL and isoform evidence. GTEx v10 provided no rs6679356–IL12RB2 sQTL, and the returned neighbouring splice association belonged to SERBP1. Transcripts lacking the canonical transmembrane segment remain annotation-level candidates, whereas reviewed truncated UniProt isoforms retain a transmembrane segment and cannot be labeled soluble receptors. **B**, Effects harmonized to the rs6679356-C PBC-risk allele across three disease datasets, OneK1K NK-cell RNA, GTEx whole-blood RNA and CSF protein. Disease and RNA effects were directionally concordant, but the measured CSF protein effect was opposite. **C**, Same-sample MESA comparison of 8,647 aligned IL12RB2 cis variants measured by Olink and SomaScan. The weak negative Pearson and Spearman correlations indicate poor cross-platform agreement. **D**, Single-causal Olink–SomaScan colocalization sensitivity across four prior specifications; all PP.H4 estimates were non-supportive. These results reject a simple RNA-to-circulating-protein-to-PBC mediation chain and do not permit CSF or circulating assay direction to be used as a proxy for cell-surface IL12RB2 pharmacology.

## Supplementary Figure S10. PXD083842 proteomic robustness and confounding

**A**, Status of TYK2 in the authors' released PBC-versus-healthy analyses. Exact log2 fold changes and FDR values are shown where publicly available; crosses indicate that TYK2 was absent from an FDR<0.05-only table and that the corresponding non-significant coefficient was not released. Absence is not interpreted as effect reversal. **B**, PBC-versus-healthy TYK2 abundance differences across parametric, non-parametric, permutation, heteroskedasticity-robust, robust-regression, winsorized and clipped analyses. **C**, Leave-one-sample-out estimates; point color denotes the group of the omitted sample. **D**, Availability and impact of prespecified confounding variables. Risk scores summarize the audit classification rather than an estimated statistical effect. The unadjusted TYK2 direction was stable to distributional assumptions and individual-sample deletion, but age and recruitment structure imposed an identification limit and prevented a PBC-specific biomarker claim.

## Supplementary Figure S11. TYK2 LD bootstrap and FIN/non-FIN sensitivity

**A**, Signed dosage-correlation distributions for rs34536443-C and rs2304256-A across 100,000 bootstrap replicates using unstratified European, population-stratified European, FIN-only and population-stratified non-FIN European reference samples. **B**, Distribution of conditional -log10(P) values obtained after propagating reference-LD sampling uncertainty through the two-variant summary-z approximation. The dashed line marks P=0.05. **C**, Observed signed correlations and non-parametric 95% bootstrap intervals; labels give the fraction of valid replicates. **D**, Percentage of replicates invalid because the rare rs34536443-C allele became monomorphic. FIN contained only three rs34536443-C alleles in the 1000 Genomes reference subset. FIN and non-FIN estimates were not significantly different, and both conditional signals remained below P=0.05 in essentially all valid replicates. These analyses propagate reference-LD uncertainty only and do not replace individual-level conditional regression or study-specific FinnGen signed LD.
