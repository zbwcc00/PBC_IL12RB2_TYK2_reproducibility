# Main Figure Legends
## Figure 1 | Single-cell eQTL-guided translational prioritization in primary biliary cholangitis

Analysis-plan-defined workflow from genetic discovery to a bounded translational hypothesis. We integrated 56 PBC GWAS loci with OneK1K single-cell eQTLs across 17 immune-cell populations. Sixteen loci had overlapping credible sets, yielding 152 locus–gene–cell screening records. Ten loci and 23 gene–cell combinations then underwent allele harmonization, signed-LD reconstruction, SuSiE fine-mapping and signal-level `coloc.susie`. Independent molecular-QTL and disease datasets were followed by donor-level liver single-cell data, bulk transcriptomics and liver proteomics. Allele direction, TYK2 functional genetics, drug and trial records, safety and counterevidence informed the final decision. Counts refer to records or tested combinations consolidated at the locus level. Selective TYK2 inhibition was CONDITIONAL GO for preclinical or early proof-of-mechanism testing. Direct IL12RB2 small-molecule docking was NO-GO under current structural data. Both classifications guide experimental prioritization rather than clinical treatment.

## Figure 2 | PBC GWAS anchoring and fine-mapping of the IL12RB2 locus

**A,** Manhattan plot of the European-ancestry PBC discovery GWAS (GCST90061440; GRCh37). The dashed line marks P = 5 × 10⁻⁸, and the lower rug marks genome-wide significant variants. IL12RB2/rs6679356, TYK2/rs2304256, HLA and selected independent peaks are annotated. TYK2 is a disease-locus anchor; cell-specific colocalization was assigned to IL12RB2. **B,** Regional association at 1p31.3. Colors show r² with rs6679356 in 503 European 1000 Genomes participants. Enlarged points mark three PBC 95% credible sets, and the lower track shows regional genes. **C,** Conditional inclusion probabilities for the three credible sets from the L=10 `susie_rss` model. Sets contained 2, 3 and 2 variants; rs6679356 had PIP = 0.940. **D,** Variant-level posterior trajectories for the first three SuSiE components. Leading variants were rs6679356, rs3828069 and rs112588573. The convergent model included 3,638 variants and used external signed LD for summary-statistic fine-mapping.

## Figure 3 | Discovery colocalization and external molecular-QTL and disease replication at IL12RB2

**A,** Discovery `coloc.susie` across three PBC and three IL12RB2/NK-cell eQTL components. PBC signal 1 shared rs6679356 with eQTL signal 1 (PP.H4 = 0.971). PP.H4 remained 0.971–0.997 across the tested prior grid. Signals 2 and 3 were not shared with the OneK1K NK-cell eQTL (maximum PP.H4 = 2.56 × 10⁻⁵ and 1.02 × 10⁻⁴, respectively). Inference used the convergent L=3 model; L=10 was non-convergent. **B,** External signal-level colocalization. Perez NK (PP.H4 = 0.9987) and GTEx whole blood (PP.H4 > 0.9999) supported PBC signal 1. Nathan Th1 provided a weaker signal 3 pairing. **C,** Effects aligned to protective rs6679356-T and FinnGen replication at variant, regional and gene levels. The IL12RB2 gene result passed BH-FDR (0.00484); the Bonferroni-adjusted P value was 0.440. **D,** FinnGen Wakefield credible sets and BF–BF sensitivity. Perez NK and GTEx whole blood were regionally compatible, whereas Nathan Th1 was unsupported. Because complete signed LD was unavailable, BF–BF provides a single-causal regional compatibility analysis rather than FinnGen `coloc.susie`. **E,** Allele-aligned disease, RNA and protein effects. rs6679356-T lowered PBC risk and IL12RB2 RNA but increased measured cerebrospinal-fluid protein, separating RNA direction from circulating-protein and cell-surface pharmacology.

## Figure 4 | Cross-platform protein boundary and TYK2 human genetic evidence

**A,** Allele-aligned IL12RB2 cis-pQTL effects for the Olink lead, SomaScan lead and PBC lead rs6679356 in the same 1,889 MESA participants. Points show estimates; error bars show 95% confidence intervals. Platform-specific assay scales are not quantitatively interchangeable. **B,** Olink and SomaScan z scores across 8,647 allele-aligned IL12RB2 cis variants. Directional agreement was 47.45%, Pearson r was −0.084 and single-causal ABF analysis gave PP.H4 ≤ 0.0166. The results identify platform-specific signals potentially shaped by epitope, fragment, protein-altering-variant or post-translational effects. **C,** Fixed-effect inverse-variance meta-analysis of TYK2 rs34536443-C in FinnGen R12 and UKB–TOPMed. FinnGen R12: OR 0.58, 95% CI 0.41–0.81, P = 1.49 × 10⁻³; UKB–TOPMed: OR 0.53, 95% CI 0.35–0.80, P = 3.20 × 10⁻³. Across 1,016 cases and 770,550 controls, OR = 0.560 (95% CI 0.432–0.727), P = 1.31 × 10⁻⁵ and I² = 0%. The genetic estimate defines direction rather than adult pharmacological exposure. **D,** Marginal and conditioned PBC z scores for rs34536443-C and rs2304256-A using European signed dosage correlation (r = 0.328). Both remained associated after two-variant approximation (P = 3.72 × 10⁻⁸ and 2.30 × 10⁻⁴); this was a summary-statistic approximation rather than individual-level COJO. **E,** Signed-LD distributions from 100,000 bootstrap draws in European, FIN and non-FIN European samples. Both conditional P values were below 0.05 in all valid European and non-FIN draws and 99.9979% of valid FIN draws. FIN had 95,233 valid draws because rs34536443-C became monomorphic in 4,767 replicates. The 1000 Genomes FIN subset quantified reference-LD uncertainty but was not FinnGen study-specific signed LD.

## Figure 5 | Liver single-cell localization of the IL12RB2–TYK2–STAT4–IFNG axis

**A,** Donor-aware fastMNN UMAP of 69,436 quality-controlled liver singlets from five PBC and five control donors. Target-independent SingleR annotation resolved major compartments and refined T/NK cells into CD4 T, CD8 T, lrNK and cNK states. **B,** Canonical-marker dot plot supporting these labels. Dot size is the expressing fraction, and color is scaled mean expression. IL12RB2, TYK2, STAT4 and IFNG were excluded from annotation features. **C,** IL12RB2 feature UMAP and target-axis dot plot. IL12RB2 was detected in 1.6% of cells; inferential tests used donors. **D,** Donor lrNK fractions did not differ by exact label permutation (P = 0.508; 252 assignments). **E,** Donor-level pseudobulk estimates across four T/NK compartments. The IL12RB2 effect was largest in CD4 T cells (log2 fold change = 2.006, P = 0.022, FDR = 0.112). The cNK estimate was 0.901 (P = 0.070, FDR = 0.474). None of the IL12RB2 contrasts passed transcriptome-wide FDR. Decreased CD8 T-cell STAT4 passed FDR (0.043); other effects were exploratory. **F,** Exact permutation and leave-one-donor-out analyses. All four IL12RB2 estimates remained positive after each donor deletion, providing stable within-cohort direction. Controls were peritumoral hemangioma liver rather than healthy-volunteer biopsies.

## Figure 6 | External single-cell resource audit and cellular-context synthesis

**A,** Within-patient antigen-context analysis in GSE270739 after collapsing repeated visits to five independent PBC patients. Paired pseudobulk IL12RB2 expression is shown for PDC-E2-reactive and pooled foreign-antigen-reactive memory CD4 T cells. The mean difference was 2.222 log2(CPM+0.5) units (95% CI −2.891 to 7.336; exact P = 0.500). Two of five patients were positive. The panel tests antigen context within PBC. **B,** Donor-paired lrNK-minus-cNK IL12RB2 contrasts in three donor-independent liver cohorts: *Journal of Hepatology* atlas (n = 4), HRA002347 5′ (n = 9) and HRA008003 (n = 10). All cohort means were positive. The HRA002347 3′ arm was excluded because it reused the nine 5′ donors. **C,** Fixed-effect and Hartung–Knapp random-effects synthesis across 23 donors. The Hartung–Knapp estimate was 1.723 (95% CI −1.637 to 5.084; one-sided P = 0.079), with I² = 98.4% and tau² = 2.536. The result supports recurrent cellular context, with high heterogeneity and a wide interval precluding definitive cross-cohort replication. **D,** Audit of public PBC single-cell resources through October 20, 2026. No additional adequately replicated public donor-level PBC–control cohort was identified. The panel distinguishes overlapping donors, technical arms, antigen- or condition-level samples and unavailable reusable matrices. PBC-versus-normal donor pseudobulk within the Andrews atlas is shown separately in Supplementary Figure S11 as external donor-level directional support from the same study-level resource.

## Figure 7 | Orthogonal molecular evidence and a safety-gated translational path for the IL12RB2–TYK2 axis

**A,** Modified Hartung–Knapp meta-analysis of analysis-plan-defined pathway scores across four PBC transcriptomic cohorts. IL12–STAT4 effects were positive in all cohorts (pooled g = 0.930, 95% CI −1.073 to 2.933; P = 0.236; FDR = 0.315; I² = 71.1%). IFNG-response effects were also positive (g = 0.765, 95% CI −0.320 to 1.849; P = 0.111; FDR = 0.221; I² = 42.8%). The programs provided positive but non-confirmatory pathway context after multiplicity adjustment. **B,** PXD083842 liver TYK2 effects for 14 PBC and 9 healthy-control samples across seven displayed representative methods; the complete eight-model audit is shown in Supplementary Figure S13. All 23 leave-one-sample-out estimates remained positive. The unadjusted log2 fold change was 0.429 (FDR = 0.011), and the cirrhosis-adjusted estimate was 0.416 (FDR = 0.016). Age and recruitment source were structurally confounded, leaving PBC-specific biomarker performance unresolved. **C,** Evidence matrix integrating targets, modalities, PBC trials, regulatory safety and genetic direction. Database presence was separated from evidence of efficacy. In NCT01389973, 0/20 participants met the alkaline-phosphatase endpoint. NCT03742973 ended after two participants because of enrollment futility. **D,** Safety and druggability gates. Deucravacitinib matched the inhibitory direction but lacked direct PBC efficacy evidence. Required safeguards included liver-enzyme, serious-infection and tuberculosis monitoring; Child–Pugh C was a red gate. Its classification was CONDITIONAL GO for preclinical or early proof-of-mechanism evaluation. IL12RB2 structures were available, but no validated small-molecule pocket, activity series or docking benchmark was identified. Direct docking was NO-GO. **E,** Final hierarchy: moderate-to-strong genetic support and a conditional, safety-gated drug hypothesis without a clinical treatment recommendation.
