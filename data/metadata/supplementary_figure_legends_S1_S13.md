## Supplementary Figure S1 | Screen-wide signal-level colocalization and model quality control

**A,** Results for all 23 analysis-plan-defined PBC locus–gene–cell combinations under the strict shared-signal prior p12 = 1 × 10⁻⁶. Point color denotes the evidence class; point size denotes the number of variants shared by the paired 95% credible sets. The non-convergent NFKB1 estimate is displayed for completeness and excluded from inference. **B,** Prior sensitivity at p12 = 1 × 10⁻⁶, 5 × 10⁻⁶ and 1 × 10⁻⁵. Evidence classes were assigned under the strict prior. **C,** Disease-side SuSiE results across 10 PBC loci. Point size denotes the number of credible sets; color denotes the minimum absolute within-set correlation. All disease models converged. Only 1p31.3 contained multiple signals, with credible sets of 2, 3 and 2 variants in the L=10 model. **D,** Model convergence. Twenty-one of 23 eQTL L=10 models converged. Analysis-plan-defined L=3 review retained the convergent IL12RB2/NK model and excluded the non-convergent NFKB1/CD4 TCM model. Under the strict prior, 6 combinations were strong, 12 candidate, 3 exploratory and 1 unsupported; 1 was excluded. Same-locus combinations were interpreted as correlated locus-level evidence.

## Supplementary Figure S2 | Local signed-LD and SuSiE quality control across 10 PBC loci

**A,** Maximum disease SuSiE posterior inclusion probabilities under L=3 and L=10. All 20 models converged, and the absolute L=10-minus-L=3 difference was ≤0.010 at every locus. **B,** The 1000 Genomes European signed dosage-correlation matrices and variant order used for fine-mapping. All 10 matrices passed file-size, finite-value, diagonal, symmetry, variant-order and allele-alignment checks. **C,** Signed-LD heatmaps for the union of L=10 credible-set variants at each locus. Blue indicates negative signed correlation. Credible-set purity uses absolute correlation and can remain high in the presence of negative signed LD. **D,** Minimum absolute within-set correlation, credible-set size, top PIP and RSS diagnostics by locus. Only 1p31.3 contained multiple signals. The 2p25.1 credible set had the lowest minimum purity (0.569); the 5q31.3, 4q24 and 7q34 sets remained broad despite relatively high purity. Thirteen small negative eigenvalues (minimum −2.14 × 10⁻⁶) were retained as numerical reference-panel diagnostics. These panels document the reference-LD basis of summary-statistic fine-mapping.

## Supplementary Figure S3 | Competing genes and locus-level downgrading

**A,** Strict-prior signal-level colocalization for analysis-plan-defined competitors at 2p25.1, 1q23.1, 18q22.2 and 17q12. ## Supplementary Figure S4 | A state-resolved secondary IL12RB2 regulatory component

**A,** Regulatory annotation of the seven variants in the three PBC IL12RB2 credible sets. Bubble size and labels show PBC posterior inclusion probability. Squares mark ENCODE distal enhancer-like signatures, and the final column gives the number of activated or stimulated T-cell enhancer-to-gene records linking the overlapping interval to IL12RB2. rs11209064 was the only credible-set variant with activated-T-cell IL12RB2 enhancer-to-gene support. **B,** Gene-level IL12RB2 `p_beta` across 56 immune-cell states. Five states had `p_beta`<0.05, all after anti-CD3/CD28 activation of CD4 T cells. The dashed line marks 0.05. **C,** IL12RB2 eQTL effects of rs11209064 aligned to the PBC risk-increasing G allele in available memory-CD4 and central-memory T-cell states. Error bars are 95% confidence intervals. Stimulated and unstimulated estimates are descriptive state contrasts, not an interaction test. **D,** 1000 Genomes European signed LD between the response-eQTL credible variants rs2201584 and rs3790561 and the PBC credible-set variants. Both response variants were strongly correlated with L2 (signed r = 0.888–0.896; r² = 0.788–0.803) but not with L1. **E,** Cross-autoimmune regional context at 1p31.3. IBD and psoriasis had genome-wide significant neighboring peaks with low LD to PBC and PP.H3≈1, supporting distinct local components. PSC and rheumatoid arthritis had no genome-wide significant regional association in the audited datasets. Displayed colocalization values use p12 = 1 × 10⁻⁵. These analyses support an activation-emergent L2 regulatory bridge and regional signal separation; they do not establish an allele-specific causal variant or a formal genotype-by-stimulation interaction.

## Supplementary Figure S5 | Independent disease replication and regional compatibility at IL12RB2

**A,** Effects aligned to the rs6679356-C PBC-risk allele across the discovery PBC GWAS, FinnGen and UKB–TOPMed. Positive coefficients consistently indicate higher PBC risk. Error bars are 95% confidence intervals; the UK Biobank standard error was reconstructed from the reported two-sided P value. This risk-allele orientation is the inverse of the protective-T orientation used in Figure 3. **B,** FinnGen replication ladder separating the analysis-plan-defined variant, regional top association and gene-level test. The gene-level test gave Z = 4.1306, P = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ and BH-FDR = 0.00484 but did not pass Bonferroni correction (adjusted P = 0.440). **C,** FinnGen 95% credible set under a single-causal Wakefield approximate-Bayes-factor model with prior standard deviation 0.20 on the log-odds scale. The 12 largest individual posterior probabilities and remaining credible-set mass are displayed. **D,** FinnGen–external-eQTL BF–BF colocalization sensitivity across three shared-signal priors, with credible-set overlap shown alongside. Perez NK and GTEx whole blood were regionally compatible; Nathan Th1 was an informative negative context. BF–BF is a single-causal approximation, not FinnGen `coloc.susie`; the public 19-variant official matrix was suitable for structural checks only.

## Supplementary Figure S6 | Splicing and cross-platform protein evidence define a mediation boundary

**A,** IL12RB2 splice-QTL and isoform findings. GTEx v10 contained no rs6679356–IL12RB2 sQTL; the neighboring splice association mapped to SERBP1. Ensembl transcripts lacking the canonical transmembrane segment remain annotation-level candidates. Reviewed truncated UniProt isoforms retain this segment and cannot be classified as validated soluble receptors. **B,** Effects aligned to the rs6679356-C PBC-risk allele across three disease datasets, OneK1K NK-cell RNA, GTEx whole-blood RNA and cerebrospinal-fluid protein. Disease and RNA effects agreed, whereas measured CSF protein had the opposite direction. **C,** Olink and SomaScan measurements for 8,647 aligned IL12RB2 cis variants in the same 1,889 MESA participants. Pearson r = −0.084 and Spearman rho = −0.070 indicated poor agreement. **D,** Single-causal Olink–SomaScan colocalization under four prior specifications; PP.H4 ranged from 0.0009 to 0.0166. The data do not fit a simple RNA-to-circulating-protein-to-PBC chain. CSF or circulating-assay direction cannot proxy cell-surface IL12RB2 pharmacology.

## Supplementary Figure S7 | TYK2 LD bootstrap and FIN/non-FIN sensitivity

**A,** Signed dosage-correlation distributions for rs34536443-C and rs2304256-A across 100,000 bootstrap replicates. Reference samples were unstratified European, population-stratified European, FIN-only and population-stratified non-FIN European. The multimodal FIN distribution reflects rare-allele resampling. **B,** Conditional -log10(P) distributions after propagating reference-LD uncertainty through the two-variant approximation. Colors distinguish rs34536443 and rs2304256; the dashed line marks P = 0.05. Both conditional P values were <0.05 in 100%, 100%, 99.9979% and 100% of valid replicates, respectively. **C,** Observed signed correlations and non-parametric 95% bootstrap intervals. Observed r values were 0.328, 0.328, 0.229 and 0.347. FIN had a 95.2% valid fraction and only three rs34536443-C alleles. **D,** Invalid replicates in which rs34536443-C became monomorphic. FIN had 4,767 invalid draws. The FIN-minus-non-FIN difference was −0.118 (95% CI −0.308 to 0.089; bootstrap P = 0.259). Intervals reflect sampling uncertainty in 1000 Genomes reference LD only. The FIN subset is not FinnGen study-specific signed LD, and this analysis does not replace individual-level conditional regression.

## Supplementary Figure S11 | External Andrews liver-atlas donor-level PBC–normal audit

**A,** Raw-count donor pseudobulk IL12RB2 in lrNK cells from the Andrews 5′ scRNA arm. With all donors containing lrNK cells, both PBC donors exceeded every normal donor (PBC n = 2, normal n = 5; mean difference 5.978 log2(CPM+0.5); exact one-sided permutation P = 0.0476). After requiring at least 10 lrNK cells per donor, only two normal donors remained eligible and the exact P value was 0.1667. **B,** The broader scRNA T/NK union retained two PBC and six normal donors after the 10-cell filter; both PBC donors exceeded all normal donors (mean difference 5.626; exact one-sided P = 0.0357). **C,** In the matched 3′ snRNA arm, the lrNK PBC–normal difference was 0.004 (P = 0.500; PBC n = 2, normal n = 3). **D,** The snRNA T/NK-union estimate after the 10-cell filter was −0.226 (P = 0.600; PBC n = 2, normal n = 3). Points represent donors, and inference used donor-level pseudobulk. With two PBC donors, uneven normal immune-cell coverage, a shared PBC001 donor and a neutral or discordant snRNA arm, the scRNA result provides external donor-level directional support rather than confirmatory differential expression.

## Supplementary Figure S13 | PXD083842 proteomic robustness and confounding

**A,** TYK2 in the released PBC-versus-healthy analyses. Exact log2 fold changes and FDR values are shown when available. Crosses indicate absence from an FDR < 0.05-only table rather than a zero or reversed effect. The unadjusted log2 fold change was 0.429 (FDR = 0.011); the cirrhosis-adjusted estimate was 0.416 (FDR = 0.016). **B,** TYK2 differences across eight parametric, non-parametric, permutation, heteroskedasticity-consistent, robust-regression, winsorized and clipped analyses. All eight estimates were positive (range 0.400–0.431). **C,** Leave-one-sample-out estimates, colored by the omitted group. All 23 remained positive. **D,** Availability and effect of analysis-plan-defined confounders. Risk scores are classifications, not estimated effects. The positive direction was insensitive to outliers and model choice, while PBC-specific biomarker performance remained unresolved.
